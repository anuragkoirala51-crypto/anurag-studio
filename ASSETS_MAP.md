## 💡 How to Replace Photos & Videos
1. **To replace photos:** Drop your image into `src/assets/images/` named `1.jpg`, `2.jpg`, etc., or edit `src/config/assets.ts`.
2. **To show / hide on screen:** A floating toggle `Markers: ON / OFF` appears on the bottom right of the website so you can review numbers or hide them anytime!
